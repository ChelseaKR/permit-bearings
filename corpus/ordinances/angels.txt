 17.61.010 Regulations for ADUs and JADUs.

 A. The city of Angels regulations for ADUs and JADUs are as set forth in California ADU law including but not limited to:

 1. Government Code Sections 66310 through 66342 ; and

 2. Government Code Sections 65852.2 , 65852.21 , 65852.22 , 65852.23 , 65852.26 , 65852.27 , 65852.150 , 65583 (c)(7), 65585 (j), 66313 , 66314 , 66322 , 66323 and 66411.7 ; and

 3. Public Resources Code Sections 21080.17 and 30500.5 ; and

 4. Health and Safety Code Sections 17980.12 and 50504.5 ; and

 5. Civil Code Sections 714.3 , 4740 , 4741 and 4751 ; 

 and as these provisions may be amended.

 B. Guidelines are available at https://www.hcd.ca.gov/building-standards/adu/handbook . (Ord. 554 (Att. A), 2026)
