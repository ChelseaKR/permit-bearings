Ordinance review aid

Review local ordinance text

Paste local ADU or SB 9 text. The prototype flags a bounded set of patterns associated with state-law conflicts documented in official sources.

This is a presence-based text screen. A flag is a review lead, not a legal conclusion. No flag does not prove that an ordinance is complete or compliant.

Text stays in your browser

Screen ordinance or handout text

The current checks cover examples such as stale SB 477 citations, some height and unit-count restrictions, subjective standards, owner-occupancy language, parking provisions, and older SB 9 fire-exclusion language.

What to expect

  • Each flag shows the matching passage, state-law basis, and documented HCD precedent.
  • The named six-provision fixture reproduces six expected review flags; it is not a statewide accuracy evaluation.
  • The pasted text is processed locally and is not saved or sent by this public demo.

Review aid only. This is not legal advice, a compliance certification, or a substitute for staff and counsel review. Silence is not a clean bill of health.
