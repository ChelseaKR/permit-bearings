Before you start

Use the information you already have. You will be asked for:

  • the city or county where the property is located;
  • the type of housing project; and
  • facts about the dwelling and proposed work.

If you do not know an answer, choose “I’m not sure.” The result will tell you what to ask staff instead of filling in the gap.

Current scope

The prototype includes statewide candidate rules for ADUs, JADUs, and SB 9. The made-up Woodland example compares reported packet facts with one source-bound City checklist. Other local information remains thin, and the Davis record reports only the City’s published processing categories and preserves HCD’s unresolved ordinance-status warning. Statewide screening does not mean a jurisdiction’s local code, forms, parcel facts, or exceptions are fully encoded.

Decision support only. This is not legal advice or a substitute for a jurisdiction’s review. Independent prototype prepared in response to the 2026 California AI Permitting Innovation Showcase challenge. Project answers are not sent to or stored by this demo.
