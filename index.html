What this prototype does not do

It does not verify parcel facts, reproduce a complete local checklist, determine final eligibility, certify completeness, or approve a permit.

Before you start

Use the information you already have. You will be asked for:

  • the city or county where the property is located;
  • the type of housing project; and
  • facts about the dwelling and proposed work.

If you do not know an answer, choose “I’m not sure.” The result will tell you what to ask staff instead of filling in the gap.

Current scope

The prototype includes statewide candidate rules for ADUs, JADUs, and SB 9. A separate made-up Woodland packet is compared with one source-bound City checklist. Other local information remains thin, and the Davis source record is explicitly unverified. Statewide screening does not mean a jurisdiction’s local code, forms, parcel facts, or exceptions are fully encoded.

Decision support only. This is not legal advice or a substitute for a jurisdiction’s review. Independent prototype prepared in response to the 2026 California AI Permitting Innovation Showcase challenge. Project answers are not sent to or stored by this demo.
