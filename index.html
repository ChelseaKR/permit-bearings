California housing permit guidance prototype

Check a California housing project

Answer questions about an ADU, JADU, or SB 9 project. The result shows candidate routes, the rules and official sources behind them, and questions to confirm with local staff.

Start a project check

No sign-in. No applicant data is stored by this public prototype.

What this prototype does not do

It does not verify parcel facts, reproduce a complete local checklist, determine final eligibility, certify completeness, or approve a permit.

Before you start

Use the information you already have. You will be asked for:

  • the city or county where the property is located;
  • the type of housing project; and
  • facts about the dwelling and proposed work.

If you do not know an answer, choose “I’m not sure.” The result will tell you what to ask staff instead of filling in the gap.

Current scope

The prototype includes statewide candidate rules for ADUs, JADUs, and SB 9. It also includes bounded local metadata for Davis and Woodland. Statewide screening does not mean a jurisdiction’s local code, forms, parcel facts, or exceptions are fully encoded.

Decision support only. This is not legal advice or a substitute for a jurisdiction’s review. Prototype for the California AI Permitting Innovation Showcase (2026). No applicant data is stored by this public demo.
